ℹ️ About CloudStore

Helping community businesses sell online

A digital marketplace for township and campus vendors in Tshwane: a prototype investigation.

❗

The problem we solve

Home cooks, small farmers, crafters and other informal vendors in Tshwane's townships and around its campuses depend on foot traffic and word of mouth, so they cannot reach customers beyond their street and lose sales to large retailers. At the same time, students and residents often travel long distances or pay high prices for everyday goods that are made close by. Most of these vendors cannot afford to build and run their own website. CloudStore solves this by giving any community vendor a ready-made online shop with delivery, so local money and local jobs stay in the community.

🎯

Aim & objectives

Aim: To design, build and host a working e-commerce prototype that lets community vendors sell online to nearby shoppers.

  1. Research at least five similar digital businesses and identify the gap CloudStore fills.
  2. Build a hosted website where shoppers can browse, search and order from at least five vendors.
  3. Calculate order totals, delivery fees and commission correctly for every order.
  4. Offer a subscription-based revenue model with at least three vendor plans and one shopper plan.
  5. Summarise sales with mean, median, range and charts on an admin dashboard.

Why it matters in South Africa

Digital entrepreneurship for sustainable community development.

💼

Jobs and income

Youth unemployment is one of South Africa's biggest challenges. Every vendor who sells more, and every delivery runner who gets work, is income earned inside the community.

🏘️

Township economies

Spending at large malls leaves the township. A local marketplace keeps that money circulating between neighbours and small businesses.

📱

Digital inclusion

Vendors build digital skills by running an online shop, without needing to code or pay for a website.

🧩

What the prototype does

  • Product catalogue with search and category filters
  • Cart with live delivery-fee calculation and free delivery over R500
  • Secure accounts (passwords are hashed, never stored as plain text)
  • Checkout with cash or card on delivery, and order history
  • Vendor plans that open a shop and let vendors add products
  • CloudStore Plus subscription for free delivery
  • CloudBot assistant for quick answers and product search
  • Admin dashboard with sales statistics and charts
🛠️

Methods & tools

Back endJava 19 (built-in HTTP server), JDBC
DatabaseMySQL 8 (users, products, orders, plans, messages)
Front endHTML5, CSS3 (glassmorphism design), JavaScript
ChartsChart.js for the dashboard and revenue projections
AssistantCloudBot, a rule-based (no-code style) assistant
SecurityPBKDF2 password hashing, HttpOnly session cookies, parameterised SQL
