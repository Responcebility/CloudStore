About us

Kasi food, done right

An online fried-chicken brand for Tshwane's townships: a prototype investigation.

😩

The problem we solve

In townships like Soshanguve, students, families and workers who want a quick, hot meal often have to take a taxi to a mall food court, or rely on delivery apps that don't always reach their streets. At the same time, kasi cooks make excellent food but can only sell to people who walk past. Kasi-Crunch fixes this with a local fried-chicken brand you order online, cooked in the kasi and delivered by local runners, so customers save time and money and the income stays in the community.

🎯

Aim & objectives

Aim: To design, build and host a working online ordering prototype for a township fried-chicken business.

  1. Study at least five similar food businesses and find the gap Kasi-Crunch fills.
  2. Build a hosted website where customers can browse the menu and place delivery orders.
  3. Calculate totals, club discounts and delivery fees correctly for every order.
  4. Offer a subscription (club) revenue model alongside food sales.
  5. Summarise sales and profit with statistics and charts on an admin dashboard.
💼

Local jobs

Every order supports cooks in the kitchen and a delivery runner from the area, which matters where youth unemployment is high.

🔁

Money stays local

Spending stays in the township instead of going to malls far away.

📱

Digital skills

Running an online food business builds digital and business skills in the community.

How we built it

Back endJava 19 (built-in HTTP server) with JDBC
DatabaseMySQL 8: customers, menu, orders, clubs and messages
Front endHTML, CSS and JavaScript, with a red-and-orange glass design
ChartsChart.js on the dashboard and the Revenue Model page
AssistantCrunchy, a rule-based (no-code style) ordering assistant
SecurityHashed passwords (PBKDF2), secure session cookies, safe database queries
TeamworkGitHub for shared code and version history
