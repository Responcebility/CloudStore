About us

Good food. Good people.

Kasi-Crunch is one kitchen, our own, serving Soshanguve and nearby. An online ordering platform for a township fast-food business: a prototype investigation.

The problem we solve

In townships like Soshanguve, students, families and workers who want a quick, hot meal often have to take a taxi to a mall food court, or rely on delivery apps that don't always reach their streets. Kasi-Crunch fixes this with our own kasi kitchen and simple online ordering: customers choose delivery, priced fairly by distance, or free collection. Regulars can buy a Crunch Pass for free delivery all month. Customers save time and money, and the income and jobs stay in the community.

Aim & objectives

Aim: To design, build and host a working online ordering prototype for a township fast-food business.

  1. Study at least five similar food businesses and find the gap Kasi-Crunch fills.
  2. Build a hosted website where customers can browse the menu and order for delivery or collection.
  3. Calculate distance-based delivery fees and Crunch Pass prices correctly.
  4. Offer a subscription (the Crunch Pass) alongside food sales.
  5. Summarise sales, profit and delivery data with statistics and charts on an admin dashboard.

Why it matters

Kasi food, kasi jobs.

Local jobs: every order supports our cooks and a delivery runner from the area, which matters where youth unemployment is high.

Money stays local: spending stays in the township instead of going to malls far away.

Digital skills: running an online business builds digital and business skills in the community.

How we built it

Back endJava 19 (built-in HTTP server) with JDBC
DatabaseMySQL 8: customers, menu, orders, Crunch Passes, messages
Front endHTML, CSS and JavaScript
ChartsChart.js on the dashboard and the Revenue Model page
AssistantCrunchy, a rule-based (no-code style) ordering assistant
SecurityHashed passwords, secure session cookies, safe database queries
TeamworkGitHub for shared code and version history
