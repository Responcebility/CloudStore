Quick answers

How much is delivery?

R16 per km, up to 30 km. Collection is free, and a Crunch Pass makes delivery free within the distance you choose.

How is the Crunch Pass priced?

R49 + R15 for every km of free delivery you choose, for 30 days. A 5 km pass is R124.

How do I pay?

Cash or card when your food arrives or when you collect.

Can I track my order?

Yes, in My account, or ask Crunchy "track my order".
