👥 Customer persona

Who we build for

CloudStore is a two-sided marketplace, so it has two target users: the shopper who buys and the vendor who sells. These personas are fictional, based on the problem we researched.

👩🏾‍🎓
Shopper persona

Thandi, the busy student

A second-year ICT student who lives in a student residence in Soshanguve and studies on a tight budget.

Age20
LocationSoshanguve, Tshwane
IncomeBursary allowance
DeviceAndroid smartphone, prepaid data

🎯 Goals

  • Affordable meals between lectures
  • Study supplies before exams
  • Save time on travel

😣 Frustrations

  • Taxi fare to the mall
  • Delivery apps that skip her area
  • Hidden fees at checkout

✅ How CloudStore helps

  • Local food and stationery
  • Delivery fee shown up front
  • Pay cash on delivery

"I just want a decent meal and my calculator delivered without spending half my allowance on transport."

👩🏿‍🍳
Vendor persona

Mama Grace, the home cook

Runs a small kitchen from her home, selling stews, vetkoek and bunny chows to neighbours and nearby workers.

Age47
LocationSoshanguve, Tshwane
BusinessInformal food vendor, 3 helpers
TechWhatsApp, basic smartphone use

🎯 Goals

  • Sell more meals every day
  • Reach students nearby
  • Hire another helper

😣 Frustrations

  • Cannot afford a website
  • Orders lost in WhatsApp chats
  • No record of her sales

✅ How CloudStore helps

  • Free Starter shop
  • Orders in one place
  • Sales and payouts tracked

"If more people could find my food, I could cook more and give work to my neighbours."

Thandi's journey on CloudStore

From landing page to a completed order.

1️⃣

Discover

Lands on the home page and sees local vendors near her residence.

2️⃣

Browse

Searches "calculator" and filters Meals for lunch.

3️⃣

Order

Sees the R35 delivery fee up front and checks out with cash on delivery.

4️⃣

Return

Tracks the order with CloudBot and joins Plus for free delivery.
