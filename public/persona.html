Why the Crunch Pass suits Thandi

Thandi lives 4 km away, so each delivery costs 4 × R16 = R64. At about 8 orders a month that is R512 in delivery fees. A 4 km Crunch Pass costs R49 + R15 × 4 = R109, which saves her R403 a month.
