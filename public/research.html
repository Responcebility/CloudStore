Market research

What we learned from five food businesses

Before building Kasi-Crunch we studied three South African chicken brands and two food-delivery apps.

🍗

Chicken Licken

SA fried-chicken chain (Chicken Licken n.d.)

👍 Does well

  • Strong, local, humorous brand
  • Affordable meals and combos

👎 Could improve

  • Stores are not on every kasi street
  • Large chain, not community-owned
🌶️

Nando's

Flame-grilled peri-peri chicken (Nando's n.d.)

👍 Does well

  • Clear spice levels on the menu
  • Recognisable brand and online ordering

👎 Could improve

  • Pricier for students
  • Mostly in malls and suburbs
🔥

Galito's

Flame-grilled chicken chain (Galito's n.d.)

👍 Does well

  • Simple menu with clear combos
  • Easy-to-read website

👎 Could improve

  • Fewer outlets in townships
  • Less known outside the cities
🛵

Mr D

Food delivery app (Mr D n.d.)

👍 Does well

  • Fast mobile ordering and live tracking
  • Fees shown before checkout

👎 Could improve

  • Coverage concentrated in urban areas
  • Fees add up on small orders
🥡

Uber Eats

Food delivery app (Uber Eats n.d.)

👍 Does well

  • Huge restaurant choice
  • Regular deals and promotions in the app

👎 Could improve

  • Service and delivery fees add up
  • Limited reach in some townships
💡

The gap Kasi-Crunch fills

Our problem statement

The big chicken brands make food people love, but mostly sell from malls and suburbs. Delivery apps make ordering easy, but their coverage and fees don't always suit township customers. Kasi-Crunch combines the two: a kasi-made chicken brand with its own simple online ordering, local delivery, clear fees and a low-cost club, built for the people who live there.

How the research shaped our prototype

  • Clear 🌶️ spicy tags on the menu, like Nando's spice levels
  • Simple combos such as the Student Saver and Family Feast, like Galito's and Chicken Licken
  • Delivery fee shown before checkout, like Mr D
  • A low-cost delivery club, priced for students and families
  • Cash or card on delivery for customers without bank cards
