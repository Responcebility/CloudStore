Revenue model

How Kasi-Crunch makes money.

One store, three income streams: e-commerce food sales, delivery fees by distance, and the Crunch Pass subscription.

🍔

Food sales

Every order earns the menu price minus what the food costs to make (the gross profit).

🚗

Delivery fees

R16 per km, up to 30 km. This pays our local delivery runners. Collection is free.

✳

Crunch Pass

A monthly subscription for free delivery. The customer picks the distance, and the distance sets the price.

Our formulas

deliveryFee = 0 (collection) = (distance − pass km) × R16 per km (delivery, never below R0) passPrice = R49 + R15 × chosen km (per month, 1–20 km) orderTotal = food subtotal + deliveryFee grossProfit = food sales − food cost margin % = grossProfit ÷ food sales × 100

Example: a Kasi Classic (R82) delivered 4.5 km costs R82 + 4.5 × R16 = R154. With a 5 km Crunch Pass (R124 a month) the same order costs just R82.
