Revenue model

How Kasi-Crunch makes money

E-commerce food sales, monthly club subscriptions, and delivery fees that pay our local runners.

🍗

Food sales

Every order earns the difference between the menu price and what the food costs to make.

⭐

Club subscriptions

Crunch Club (R49) and Family Club (R129) bring in steady income every month.

🚚

Delivery fees

R25 on orders up to R250 helps pay local delivery runners.

Join a club

Log in, pick a club, and your benefits start on your next order. Cancel any time.

Our formulas

discount = subtotal × club discount (0% or 10%) delivery = R0 if in a club or subtotal > R250 = R25 otherwise orderTotal = subtotal − discount + delivery grossProfit = food sales − food cost margin % = grossProfit ÷ food sales × 100

Example: a 4-Piece Bucket (R89.90) costs R89.90 + R25 = R114.90. A Family Club member pays R89.90 − R8.99 = R80.91, with free delivery.
