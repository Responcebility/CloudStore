💰 Revenue model

How CloudStore makes money

A hybrid e-commerce and subscription model: vendors pay a small commission when they sell and can subscribe for lower rates, shoppers can subscribe for free delivery, and delivery fees cover local delivery runners.

🧾

Sales commission

3% to 10% of each sale, depending on the vendor's plan. We only earn when vendors earn.

🏪

Vendor plans

Growth (R149) and Pro (R349) monthly plans trade a fixed fee for lower commission and more features.

⭐

CloudStore Plus

Shoppers pay R49 a month for free delivery on every order: steady recurring income.

🚚

Delivery fees

R35 per order under R500, which pays community delivery runners.

The formulas we use

Every order total is calculated on the server with these rules (see Pricing.java).

deliveryFee = 0 if subtotal > R500 or shopper has Plus = R35 otherwise orderTotal = subtotal + deliveryFee commission = subtotal × vendor plan rate payout = subtotal − commission

Example: a R180 veggie box from a Starter vendor costs the shopper R180 + R35 = R215. CloudStore keeps R180 × 10% = R18, and the vendor receives R162.
